Exiting the Hero Trap

Shifting from Technical Expert to Post-Heroic Leader
Braz Brandt · Technology Director & Director of Delivery Operations, Cigna

Most leaders get promoted into management because they were excellent individual contributors — then get little to no training in how to actually lead people. This talk names that trap, walks through the inner work of shifting from heroic to post-heroic leadership, and covers the practices that help it stick: two lenses to run before you act, and language for handing work back on purpose.
