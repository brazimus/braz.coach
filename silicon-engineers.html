Directing Silicon Engineers

What Product Management Still Buys You When the Engineer Is Made of Silicon
Braz Brandt · Technology Director & Director of Delivery Operations, Cigna

Agentic AI tools — Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity — don't just answer a question, they take dozens of steps toward a goal before checking back in, and this talk calls that a "silicon engineer." It argues that when the engineer stops being human, the PM/PO's job doesn't disappear — some of it is skill you already have, and some of it is genuinely new muscle. The evidence runs across three personal projects and a Fortune 15 delivery org, run by the same person, testing the same patterns at very different scale.
